## Short-form editing guides

- Clipfox short-form editing guide: 3-second hooks, multi-pass editing, muted mobile audits, B-roll discipline
- BlitzReels short-form editing guide: cut on vocal emphasis, framing shifts, caption rhythm, purposeful B-roll
- Splice, "Mastering Short-Form Video Editing": jump cuts, 1–3 s shot pacing, text overlays, audio
- White Wolf Marketing short-form best practices: 5–10-word hooks, transitions, pacing
- Wisecut AI clipping guides: long-form to vertical shorts
- Cliptalk, "Best Free YouTube Shorts Makers": manual and AI-assisted tools

## Hooks and retention

- GEN.pro top 10 hooks: failure-first, before/after gaps, contrarian takes, one edit into five hook variations
- Klap, 8 social media hooks + retention benchmarks: completion by duration; 3-second retention 45% weak vs 55%+ strong
- r/socialmedia "5 Hook Formulas": Pointed Truth, Micro-Scenario, Fast Reward, You vs. Problem, Clean Curiosity Gap

## AI clipping platforms (competitive reference)

- Loopdesk, "AI Video Understanding Explained": 1 FPS frame sampling, audio features, diarization vs transcription, agentic timeline exploration
- OpusClip / ClipAnything: multi-genre highlight detection, reframing, virality scoring
- quso.ai: virality scoring, editable transcript workflow, multi-platform resizing
- OpenShorts: open-source + cloud clipper; comparison vs OpusClip, Klap, Vizard, Kapwing

## Open-source clippers (GitHub)

- [artbyjazi/autoclip](https://github.com/artbyjazi/autoclip): WhisperX, Pyannote diarization, LLM word-index highlight detection, shot-aware vertical reframing, libass captions
- [Steezy-code/clipper](https://github.com/Steezy-code/clipper): faster-whisper word timestamps, local Qwen3 scoring, OpenCV YuNet face tracking, FFmpeg
- [ClipsAI/clipsai](https://github.com/ClipsAI/clipsai): Python library; WhisperX + Pyannote segmentation, 16:9 → 9:16 resizing
- [sherlock-dev-ai/Clipify-AI-Video-Clipper-FREE](https://github.com/sherlock-dev-ai/Clipify-AI-Video-Clipper-FREE): livestream monitoring, face-tracking crop, animated word captions
- [Cstrp/vml](https://github.com/Cstrp/vml): whisper.cpp, Kokoro TTS, FFmpeg behind a REST API
- [viddotech/videoalchemy](https://github.com/viddotech/videoalchemy): YAML workflows chaining Whisper output into FFmpeg cuts

The full list of useful repos by pipeline stage is in [repos.md](repos.md).
